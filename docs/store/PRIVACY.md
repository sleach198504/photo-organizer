# Photo Organizer privacy policy

*Draft for publishing at a public web address, which the Microsoft Store requires. Replace the
contact line before publishing.*

**Photo Organizer does not collect, send or share any information about you or your photos.**

## What it does with your photos

Photo Organizer reads the photos and videos in the folders you choose, on your own computer, to find
duplicates, blurry shots and screenshots, and to recognise what is in a picture so you can search for it.
All of that happens on your computer. Your photos, their contents, and the results are never uploaded
anywhere.

The recognition model is part of the app. It runs on your computer and needs no internet connection.

## What it stores

It keeps a list of your photos (file names, folders, dates, and the results of its analysis), your settings,
and small preview images, in a folder in your user profile (`%LocalAppData%\Photo Organizer`). This
information stays on your computer. Removing the app removes it. Your photos themselves are only moved
when you tell the app to, and only into a "review" folder you can see; nothing is deleted until you
confirm it.

## What it sends

Nothing. The Microsoft Store version makes no network requests of its own; Microsoft delivers updates
through the Store.

The version installed with `Install.bat` can check GitHub for a newer release, only when you press
"Check for updates". That request contains no information about you or your photos.

## Third parties

Place names for trips come from a database bundled with the app, so no map service is contacted.
Photo Organizer includes no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
